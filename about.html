OUR ORIGIN

Built by someone who knows both sides of the treatment room.

Good information should be useful to everyone in this industry, from the patient choosing a clinic to the companies that support it.

SkinDay was founded by a cosmetic physician with more than 16 years of experience in aesthetic medicine across Greater China and North America: treating patients, working with clinics, and training practitioners on energy-based technologies.

From that vantage point, one pattern became impossible to ignore: patients needed clearer choices, clinics needed better ways to demonstrate their value, and the companies serving them needed more visibility into a fragmented market.

SkinDay began with the belief that the industry works better when useful information is easier to find, compare and act on. The platform has evolved, but that idea remains the same.
